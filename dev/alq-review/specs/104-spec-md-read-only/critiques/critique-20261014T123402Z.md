## Executive Summary

The 2026-10-14 narrowing makes the design simpler and safer. The diff check at launch (US4) is now the one gate, and the
edit-tool rule (US1) only refuses earlier. The gate no longer rebases or pushes, so the previous critique's E1, E3, E4, E7
and P4–P6 are resolved or moot. Two issues remain. First, `tasks.md` was generated from the earlier design and contradicts
the current spec. Second, FR-010's fresh measurement after a rebase is keyed to the `rebase <ref>` keyword. The spec's own
US1 and US4 next steps tell the agent to rebase without that keyword, so those rounds would get a delta from a head that is
no longer an ancestor of HEAD.
